# IAPQB Reports

Research reports by the International AI Practice Qualification Board (IAPQB) on how courts, regulators, companies and researchers deal with AI at work, with the data behind them.

- Web versions: https://iapqb.org/research/
- DOI for the series (all releases): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24248199 · release 1 (Reports 01 to 04): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24248200
- Each report sets out a problem, the documented cases behind it and what practitioners now do about it. Every source is opened and checked before publication, and anything that cannot be checked is left out. AI tools were used to search and to draft; IAPQB checked every claim against its source and is responsible for the text.
- Licence: report text CC BY 4.0. Quoted material stays with its owners. The data in `data/` is aggregated from a Korean government public release; see `data/README.md`.

## About IAPQB

IAPQB (국제AI실무자격검정원) is a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea. It runs IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI, a private qualification registered under the Framework Act on Qualifications of the Republic of Korea (registration No. 2026-008253). It is not a national qualification or a government-accredited private qualification. Teaching and testing are kept apart: IAPQB does not sell courses, textbooks or materials.
